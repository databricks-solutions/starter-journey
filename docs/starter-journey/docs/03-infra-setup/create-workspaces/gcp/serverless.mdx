# GCP serverless workspace creation

> **You'll create** a serverless Databricks workspace on GCP in ~10 min.
>
> **Prereqs:** [GCP account](/docs/02-before-you-start/cloud-tenant-ready/single-tenant-setup), [Databricks account console](/docs/02-before-you-start/foundations/account-console)

## The big picture

A running Databricks workspace where Databricks manages the compute and the storage.
There is no VPC to size, no service account to wire up, and no cross-project trust to configure.
You turn on a preview, fill in a short dialog, grant Google account permissions, and the workspace comes up ready to use.
Start here for your first GCP workspace and for most new deployments.

## Prerequisites

- A Databricks account with account-admin privileges (GCP Marketplace subscription).
- A GCP region where serverless workspaces are available.
Deploy the workspace close to your data sources.

## Steps

<StepGuide
  description="Follow along in the account console. Every step links to itself, so you can send someone straight to the one they are stuck on."
  minutes={10}
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/gcp/en/admin/account-settings-e2/workspaces.html"
  referenceLabel="Databricks workspace administration on GCP (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Go to the [Databricks account console](https://accounts.gcp.databricks.com) and log in.
Check the account name in the top left and confirm you are in the account you mean to deploy into.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/1.png" alt="Databricks account portal log in screen with email field and Continue button" />

</Step>

<Step>

In the left sidebar, open **Previews**.
Find **Serverless Workspaces** and turn its toggle to **On**.

This is what makes the serverless option appear in the create dialog.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/2.png" alt="Previews page in the GCP account console with the Serverless Workspaces public preview toggle set to On" />

</Step>

<Step>

In the left sidebar, open **Workspaces**.
Click **Create workspace** in the top right.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/3.png" alt="Workspaces page in the GCP account console listing existing workspaces and the Create workspace button in the top right" />

</Step>

<Step>

In the **Create Workspace** dialog:

- Enter a **Workspace name**.
- Pick the **Region** to deploy into.
You cannot change this later.
- Choose a **Metastore** if one already exists in that region.
This is optional.
- Under **Storage and compute**, select **Use serverless compute with default storage**.
- Click **Create**.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/4.png" alt="Create Workspace dialog showing workspace name, region, metastore, and the Use serverless compute with default storage option selected" />

</Step>

<Step>

The workspace configuration page opens with the status **Running**.
The metastore, region, and network policy are shown.
Click **Open workspace**.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/5.png" alt="Workspace configuration page showing region, metastore, network policy, and a Running workspace status with the Open workspace button" />

</Step>

<Step>

A Google **Sign in with Google** screen appears.
Select the same Google account you used to log in to the Databricks account console.
Click **Allow** to grant Databricks the necessary GCP permissions.

This step is specific to GCP.
Clicking **Allow** lets Databricks manage your Google Cloud resources on your behalf.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/6.png" alt="Google Sign in with Google screen showing Databricks wants to access your Google Account, with Cancel and Allow buttons" />

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Verify

You land in the workspace itself, not the account console.

The **Welcome to Databricks** home loads with the left navigation for Workspace, Catalog, Compute, and SQL.
Seeing this page means the workspace is deployed and ready.

<StepImage src="/img/gcp-serverless/7.png" alt="Welcome to Databricks workspace home for the new GCP serverless workspace, showing the full left navigation" />

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>Serverless Workspaces is not in the Previews list</summary>

The preview toggle only shows for account admins.
Confirm you have account-admin privileges on the account console.
If it is still missing, serverless workspaces may not be available yet in your account or region.
Check the [supported GCP regions](https://docs.databricks.com/gcp/en/resources/supported-regions) list.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Wrong Google account on the Allow step</summary>

The Google account you click **Allow** with must match the account linked to your Databricks account.
If you see a different account in the sign-in prompt, click **Use a different account** and sign in with the correct one.
Allowing with the wrong account causes permission errors when the workspace tries to access GCP resources.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Picked the wrong region</summary>

A workspace cannot move regions once created.
Delete the workspace and create a new one in the correct region.
Deploy in the region that hosts your data sources to avoid cross-region egress fees.
See [supported GCP regions](https://docs.databricks.com/gcp/en/resources/supported-regions) for the full list.
</details>
