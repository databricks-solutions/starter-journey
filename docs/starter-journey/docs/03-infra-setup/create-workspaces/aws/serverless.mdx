# AWS serverless workspace creation

> **You'll create** a serverless Databricks workspace on AWS in ~10 min.
>
> **Prereqs:** [AWS account](/docs/02-before-you-start/cloud-tenant-ready/single-tenant-setup), [Databricks account console](/docs/02-before-you-start/foundations/account-console)

## The big picture

A running Databricks workspace where Databricks manages the compute and the storage.
There is no VPC to size, no IAM role to wire up, and no cross-account trust to configure.
You turn on a preview, fill in a short dialog, and the workspace comes up ready to use.
Start here for your first workspace and for most new deployments.

## Prerequisites

- A Databricks account with account-admin privileges.
- An AWS region where serverless workspaces are available. Deploy the workspace close to your data sources.

## Steps

<StepGuide
  description="Follow along in the account console. Every step links to itself, so you can send someone straight to the one they are stuck on."
  minutes={10}
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/admin/account-settings-e2/workspaces.html"
  referenceLabel="Databricks workspace administration (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Go to the [Databricks account console](https://accounts.cloud.databricks.com) and log in.
Check the account name in the top left and confirm you are in the account you mean to deploy into.

<StepImage src="/img/aws-serverless/1.png" alt="Databricks account console log in screen" />

</Step>

<Step>

In the left sidebar, open **Previews**.
Find **Serverless Workspaces** and turn its toggle to **On**.

This is what makes the serverless option appear in the create dialog.

<StepImage src="/img/aws-serverless/2.png" alt="Previews page with the Serverless Workspaces public preview toggled on" />

</Step>

<Step>

In the left sidebar, open **Workspaces**.
Click **Create workspace** in the top right.

<StepImage src="/img/aws-serverless/3.png" alt="Workspaces page in the account console with the Create workspace button" />

</Step>

<Step>

In the **Create Workspace** dialog:

- Enter a **Workspace name**.
- Pick the **Region** to deploy into. You cannot change this later.
- Under **Storage and compute**, select **Use serverless compute with default storage**.
- Choose a **Metastore** if one already exists in that region. This is optional.
- Click **Create**.

<StepImage src="/img/aws-serverless/4.png" alt="Create Workspace dialog with the serverless compute with default storage option selected" />

</Step>

<Step>

The workspace configuration page opens and the status moves to **Running**.
Storage shows as managed, and the network policy is attached automatically.
Click **Open workspace**.

<StepImage src="/img/aws-serverless/5.png" alt="New workspace configuration page showing managed storage, a network policy, and a running status" />

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Verify

You land in the workspace itself, not the account console.

The **Welcome to Databricks** home loads with the left navigation for Workspace, Catalog, Compute, and SQL.
Seeing this page means the workspace is deployed and ready.

<StepImage src="/img/aws-serverless/6.png" alt="Welcome to Databricks workspace home for the new serverless workspace" />

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>Serverless Workspaces is not in the Previews list</summary>

The preview toggle only shows for account admins.
Confirm you have account-admin privileges on the account console.
If it is still missing, serverless workspaces may not be available yet in your account or region.
</details>

<details>
<summary>The Metastore dropdown is empty</summary>

The metastore is optional at creation time.
Leave it blank if no metastore exists in that region, then attach one later.
See [Set the metastore admin group](/docs/03-infra-setup/metastore-admins/set-admin-group) for managing metastore ownership.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Picked the wrong region</summary>

A workspace cannot move regions once created.
Delete the workspace and create a new one in the correct region.
Deploy in the region that hosts your data sources to avoid cross-region egress fees.
</details>
