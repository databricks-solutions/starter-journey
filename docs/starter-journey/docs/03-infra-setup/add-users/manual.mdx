# Add Users: Manual

> **You'll add** users to the Databricks account through the console in ~2 min per user.
>
> **Prereqs:** [Account Console](/docs/02-before-you-start/foundations/account-console), account-admin privileges

## The big picture

Users registered at the account level, ready to assign to any workspace.

## Prerequisites

- Account-admin access to the Databricks account console.

## Steps

<StepGuide
  description="Register users at the account level through the console. You assign them to workspaces and groups later."
  minutes={2}
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/admin/users-groups/"
  referenceLabel="Add users to your account (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Open the account console for your cloud

- [AWS](https://accounts.cloud.databricks.com)
- [Azure](https://accounts.azuredatabricks.net)
- [GCP](https://accounts.gcp.databricks.com)

</Step>

<Step>

In the left panel, click **User management**. On the **Users** tab, click **Add user**, enter the user's **email** and **full name**, then click **Add**. The user now shows up in the Users list.

<StepImage src="/img/account-users.png" placeholder="Account console user management screen" />

</Step>

<Step>

Add everyone who needs access. You assign them to workspaces and groups in the next steps, so for now you are just getting the names into the account.

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Verify

<StepGuide eyebrow="Verify" idPrefix="verify">

<Step>

Go to **User management** > **Users**.

</Step>

<Step>

Check that each new user is in the list with the right email.

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>User already exists error</summary>

The email is already in the account. Search the Users tab for it: a previous admin may have added that person by hand, or a SCIM sync already pulled them in.
</details>
