# Domains, subdomains, and pages

> **You'll build** a published Bakehouse ontology that routes overview questions to a dashboard and detailed questions to a Genie Agent in ~30 min.
>
> **Prereqs:** [Metric Views](/docs/05-genie-ontology/metric-views), [Dashboards](/docs/05-genie-ontology/dashboards), and [Genie Agents](/docs/05-genie-ontology/genie-agents) in this section

## The big picture

You will create one domain with a direct dashboard page and a Sales Analytics subdomain with a Genie Agent page.
After publishing the full tree, Genie One can choose the right page from the wording of a question.

## Create the domain

<StepGuide
  description="Create the Bakehouse Sales Operations domain and leave it unpublished while you add its routing tree."
  minutes={5}
  idPrefix="create-domain"
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/discover/"
  referenceLabel="Discover data and AI assets (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

In the workspace sidebar, click **Discover**.
Click **Create**, then choose **Create domain**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/domain-01.png" alt="Discover page with Create domain selected from the Create menu" />

</Step>

<Step>

Complete the domain:

- **Title:** `Bakehouse Sales Operations`
- **Subtitle:** `Domain for Bakehouse Business Unit`
- **Description:** `Central domain for all Bakehouse franchise sales analytics. Covers revenue tracking, order volume, average order value, and performance comparisons across 46 franchise locations and 6 product lines.`
- **Technical contacts:** Select the Databricks engineers who maintain the domain and its assets.
- **Business contacts:** Select the target business users or downstream users.

Click **Create**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/domain-02.png" alt="Create domain dialog completed for Bakehouse Sales Operations with technical and business contacts" />

</Step>

<Step>

Confirm that the Bakehouse Sales Operations domain opens in a draft state.
Do not publish it yet.
Its pages and subdomain need to be in place before users can rely on the routing tree.

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Create the domain page

<StepGuide
  description="Add a dashboard page for overview and snapshot questions."
  minutes={7}
  idPrefix="create-domain-page"
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/discover/"
  referenceLabel="Discover data and AI assets (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Inside Bakehouse Sales Operations, click **Create**, then choose **Create page**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/domain-page-01.png" alt="Bakehouse Sales Operations domain with Create page selected" />

</Step>

<Step>

Name the page `Franchise Performance at a Glance`.
Add synonyms such as `analytics` and `numbers`.

Use this description:

```text
Visual dashboard showing KPI counters, time-series trends, and franchise/product breakdowns. Start here for a quick pulse check on sales performance.
```

Keep the Markdown page body focused on routing:

```md
## When to route here

Use this page when someone asks for an overview, snapshot, high-level summary, or visual comparison of franchise sales.

## Example questions

- Show me total sales this month.
- Which franchise has the highest revenue?
- How do products compare?
```

Under **Related Assets**, add the existing `Bakehouse Franchise Performance` dashboard.
Add the same dashboard under **Sources**, then click **Publish**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/domain-page-02.png" alt="Franchise Performance at a Glance page with synonyms, routing text, dashboard asset, source, and Publish button" />

</Step>

<Step>

On the published page, find the **Discussion** area.
Databricks engineers can use it to discuss whether content should be added, edited, or removed.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/domain-page-03.png" alt="Published Franchise Performance at a Glance page with the Discussion area highlighted" />

</Step>

<Step>

Return to Bakehouse Sales Operations and confirm that **Franchise Performance at a Glance** appears under **Pages**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/domain-page-04.png" alt="Bakehouse Sales Operations domain showing Franchise Performance at a Glance under Pages" />

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Create the subdomain page

<StepGuide
  description="Add a Genie Agent page for rankings, comparisons, and detailed analysis."
  minutes={6}
  idPrefix="create-subdomain-page"
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/discover/"
  referenceLabel="Discover data and AI assets (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Inside Sales Analytics, click **Create page**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/subdomain-page-01.png" alt="Sales Analytics subdomain with the Create page button highlighted" />

</Step>

<Step>

Name the page `Sales Q&A Assistant`.
Add a synonym such as `Chatbot`.

Use this description:

```text
Conversational AI agent that answers free-form questions about franchise sales. Supports trend analysis, comparisons, rankings, and daily patterns using natural language.
```

Keep the Markdown page body focused on the questions this page handles:

```md
## Definition

Use this page for ad hoc questions, rankings, comparisons, trends, and analysis of a specific franchise or product.

## Business use

- What was Baked Bliss's total sales on May 12?
- Rank the top 5 franchises by order count.
- Compare Golden Gate Ginger with Pearly Pies across all franchises.
```

Under **Related Assets**, add the existing `Bakehouse Sales Assistant` Genie Agent.
Add the same agent under **Sources**, then click **Publish**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/subdomain-page-02.png" alt="Sales Q&A Assistant page with routing text, Genie Agent asset, source, and Publish button" />

</Step>

<Step>

Confirm that the published Sales Q&A Assistant page shows the description, body, related Genie Agent, and source.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/subdomain-page-03.png" alt="Published Sales Q&A Assistant page with the Bakehouse Sales Assistant asset and source" />

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Publish and test the ontology

<StepGuide
  description="Publish the completed domain, then test the dashboard and agent routes in Genie One."
  minutes={7}
  idPrefix="publish-test"
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/discover/"
  referenceLabel="Discover data and AI assets (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Return to Bakehouse Sales Operations.
Confirm that its page and Sales Analytics subdomain are present, then click **Publish**.

The domain stayed unpublished until now because its routing tree was incomplete.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-01.png" alt="Bakehouse Sales Operations domain with Sales Analytics visible and Publish highlighted" />

</Step>

<Step>

Open the app switcher in the upper-right corner and select **Genie One**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-02.png" alt="Databricks app switcher with Genie One selected" />

Start from the Genie One prompt.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-03.png" alt="Genie One home page with the question prompt ready" />

</Step>

<Step>

`[For the Bakehouse Starter Journey Context]` is included because the demo workspace contains hundreds of domains and pages.
An end user in a normal environment does not need to add it.

Paste the first test:

<PromptBlock>
[For the Bakehouse Starter Journey Context]

Show me a high-level snapshot of how our franchise sales are performing this month.
</PromptBlock>

The words "high-level snapshot" route the question to the dashboard page, which is built for visual KPI summaries.
During the response, Genie reads the Bakehouse Franchise Performance dashboard and the shared metric view.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-04.png" alt="Genie One routing the high-level snapshot question to the dashboard and metric view" />

Review the dashboard-backed summary, then open **Thought process**.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-05.png" alt="Genie One result showing franchise sales KPIs and the Thought process control" />

The thought process shows the dashboard and `franchise_sales_metrics` as the selected sources.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-06.png" alt="Genie One thought process showing the dashboard and franchise_sales_metrics sources" />

</Step>

<Step>

Start a new chat and paste the second test:

<PromptBlock>
[For the Bakehouse Starter Journey Context]

Which 5 franchises had the lowest total sales, and how does their average order value compare to the network average?
</PromptBlock>

The ranking and computed comparison require an ad hoc query.
Genie routes the question through Sales Analytics to the Bakehouse Sales Assistant Genie Agent.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-07.png" alt="Genie One routing the lowest-sales comparison question to the Sales Q&A Assistant" />

Review the five franchises and their average order values.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-08.png" alt="Genie One result listing the five lowest-sales franchises and average order value comparison" />

Scroll to inspect the comparison chart.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-09.png" alt="Genie One comparison chart for franchise average order value and network average" />

Open **Thought process**.
It should identify the Bakehouse Sales Assistant and `franchise_sales_metrics` before delegating the question to the agent.

<StepImage src="/img/genie-ontology/publish-test-10.png" alt="Genie One thought process showing the Bakehouse Sales Assistant and franchise_sales_metrics route" />

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Verify

- Bakehouse Sales Operations is published and contains the Franchise Performance at a Glance page plus the Sales Analytics subdomain.
- Sales Analytics contains the published Sales Q&A Assistant page.
- The overview prompt reads the dashboard and metric view.
- The ranking prompt delegates to the Genie Agent.

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>The domain does not appear in Genie One</summary>

Confirm that you published the two pages and the parent domain.
A draft domain is not available for normal routing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>The overview question routes to the agent</summary>

Check the Franchise Performance at a Glance description and body for the words `overview`, `snapshot`, `high-level summary`, and `visual comparison`.
Those terms distinguish the dashboard page from detailed analysis.
</details>

<details>
<summary>The detailed question routes to the dashboard</summary>

Check that Sales Q&A Assistant describes rankings, comparisons, trends, and specific franchise or product analysis.
Also confirm that the Bakehouse Sales Assistant is present in both **Related Assets** and **Sources**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>The two routes return different KPI values</summary>

Confirm that the dashboard and Genie Agent both use `starterjourney_catalog.bakehouse_gold.franchise_sales_metrics`.
The ontology chooses a surface, but the metric view keeps the definitions consistent.
</details>
