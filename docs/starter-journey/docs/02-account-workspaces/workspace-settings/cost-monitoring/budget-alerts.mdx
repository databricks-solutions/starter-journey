# Budget alerts

> **You'll configure** account-level budgets that email stakeholders when spend crosses list-price thresholds.
>
> **Prereqs:** [Account Console foundations](/docs/01-introduction/foundations/account-console)

## The big picture

Monthly **USD** budgets driven by **list prices**, platform add-ons included. When spend crosses a threshold, the budget emails the recipients you name. It never throttles a workload.

## Prerequisites

- **Account admin** role.
- Optional: live **custom tags** if you want to filter budgets per team ([Tags and attribution](/docs/02-account-workspaces/workspace-settings/cost-monitoring/tag-compute-and-jobs)).

:::warning

Only **account admins** create budgets. A budget only watches. Usage keeps accruing after you cross a threshold.

:::

Budgets are in **Public Preview**, so the limits may change before GA.

## Steps

<StepGuide
  description="Create an account budget for a development workspace and add two email thresholds."
  minutes={10}
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/admin/account-settings/budgets"
  referenceLabel="Create and monitor budgets (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Sign in as an account admin:

- **AWS:** [accounts.cloud.databricks.com](https://accounts.cloud.databricks.com)
- **Azure:** [accounts.azuredatabricks.net](https://accounts.azuredatabricks.net)
- **GCP:** [accounts.gcp.databricks.com](https://accounts.gcp.databricks.com)

</Step>

<Step>

Open **Usage** > **Budgets**, then click **Add budget**.

</Step>

<Step>

Set **Budget name** to `Development Workspace budget`.
Under **Definitions**, restrict the budget to your non-production workspace.
Add tag filters if you configured them on [Tags and attribution](/docs/02-account-workspaces/workspace-settings/cost-monitoring/tag-compute-and-jobs).

</Step>

<Step>

Add **$500** and **$1000** as separate monthly alert thresholds.
Enter the distribution list that should receive each alert, then save the budget.

<StepImage src="/img/create-budget.webp" alt="Create budget form showing name, workspace scope, and two monthly alert thresholds at $500 and $1000" />

</Step>

</StepGuide>

Expect some lag.
Alerts can trail actual usage by up to **24 hours**, and a brand-new budget may show **$0** for a while as telemetry catches up.

## Verify

1. **Usage** → **Budgets** lists the new budget.
2. Open it. Cumulative spend plots against the dotted threshold lines.
3. To force a dry-run email, set a threshold below month-to-date spend and wait. Check spam.

<StepImage src="/img/verify-budget.webp" alt="Budget detail panel showing the Development Workspace budget with monthly thresholds and alert email addresses" />

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>Budget stuck at zero</summary>

Wait up to a day for telemetry, and confirm the account actually generated usage this month.

</details>

<details>
<summary>No email</summary>

Check the addresses and the spam folders, and confirm spend crossed the threshold once you account for the latency.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Totals mismatch invoices</summary>

Budgets ignore credits and discounts on purpose. Model your contract in SQL or an external tool if you need invoice-accurate thresholds.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Cannot create budgets</summary>

Only **account admins** manage budgets.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Tag filters empty</summary>

Confirm the resources emitted that tag before the budget month ([Tags and attribution](/docs/02-account-workspaces/workspace-settings/cost-monitoring/tag-compute-and-jobs)).

</details>
