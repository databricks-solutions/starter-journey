# Add Groups: Manual

> **You'll create** groups in the Databricks account console and assign users in ~5 min.
>
> **Prereqs:** [Add Users](/docs/02-account-workspaces/add-users/manual), [Groups](/docs/04-governance/groups)

## The big picture

Account-level groups with members assigned, ready to grant permissions against in any workspace.

## Prerequisites

- Account-admin access to the Databricks account console.
- Users already added (see [Add Users](/docs/02-account-workspaces/add-users/manual)).

## Steps

<StepGuide
  description="Create account-level groups in the console and assign the users you already added. Repeat for the full set your org needs."
  minutes={5}
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/admin/users-groups/"
  referenceLabel="Manage groups (Databricks docs)">

<Step>

Open the account console for your cloud

- [AWS](https://accounts.cloud.databricks.com)
- [Azure](https://accounts.azuredatabricks.net)
- [GCP](https://accounts.gcp.databricks.com)

</Step>

<Step>

In the left panel, click **User management**. On the **Groups** tab, click **Add group**, enter the group name (for example, `data-engineers` or `dev-ws-admins`), then click **Add Group**.

</Step>

<Step>

Click the group you just made, click **Add members**, pick the users you added earlier, then click **Add**.

</Step>

<Step>

Repeat for each group your org needs. The [standard data personas](/docs/04-governance/groups) list the recommended ones.

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Verify

<StepGuide eyebrow="Verify" idPrefix="verify">

<Step>

Go to **User management** > **Groups**.

</Step>

<Step>

Open each group and confirm the right people are in it.

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>Group not visible in a workspace</summary>

An account-level group has to be assigned to a workspace before it shows up there. In the account console, open the workspace settings and add the group under **Permissions**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Cannot add a user to a group</summary>

The user has to exist at the account level first. Check that they appear under **User management** > **Users** before you try to add them.
</details>
