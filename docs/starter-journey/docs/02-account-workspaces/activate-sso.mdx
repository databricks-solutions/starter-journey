# Activate SSO

> **You'll configure** SSO so users sign in to Databricks with your identity provider in ~10 min.
>
> **Prereqs:** [Add Users](/docs/02-account-workspaces/add-users/manual), [Add Groups](/docs/02-account-workspaces/add-groups/manual), identity provider admin access

## The big picture

Single sign-on at the Databricks account level. Users sign in through your existing identity provider, so there are no separate Databricks passwords to manage or revoke.

## Prerequisites

- Account-admin access to the Databricks account console.
- Admin access to your identity provider (Entra ID, Okta, or another SAML/OIDC provider).

## Steps

<StepGuide
  description="Choose your cloud path, configure the identity provider when required, then test the redirect."
  minutes={10}
  referenceHref="https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/security/auth/single-sign-on/"

<Step>

Choose the path for your cloud:

- **Azure:** Nothing to configure.
  Entra ID SSO is on by default for Azure Databricks.
- **AWS:** Open [Configure SSO for AWS](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/security/auth/single-sign-on/), configure the SAML or OIDC application in your identity provider, then register it under **Settings** > **Single sign-on** in the account console.
- **GCP:** Google SSO is often on already.
  If it is not, open [Configure SSO for GCP](https://docs.databricks.com/gcp/en/security/auth/single-sign-on/), configure the SAML or OIDC application in your identity provider, then register it in the account console.

</Step>

<Step>

Open a private or incognito browser window and go to the workspace URL.
Confirm the login redirects to your identity provider, then sign in and confirm you land in the workspace.

</Step>

</StepGuide>

## Verify

1. Log in to a workspace through SSO from a private browser window.
2. In the account console, open **Settings** > **Single sign-on** and confirm the configuration shows as active.

## Where people trip

<details>
<summary>SSO redirect fails or loops</summary>

Check the SAML/OIDC reply URL configured in your IdP. It must match the URL registered in the Databricks account console exactly. Trailing slashes and protocol mismatches are common causes.
</details>

<details>
<summary>User authenticates but cannot access a workspace</summary>

SSO handles authentication, not authorization. The user must also be assigned to the workspace. Verify their assignment in the account console under the workspace's permission settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Azure: Entra ID SSO not working</summary>

Entra ID SSO is enabled by default, but the user must exist in the linked Entra ID directory. If using a guest account or B2B user, verify the account has been invited and accepted.
</details>
